# KinBech — Functionality

**App name:** KinBech  
**Tagline:** Buy. Sell. Save.

KinBech is a local second-hand marketplace. People find used items nearby, post their own ads, chat to negotiate, and meet in person to complete a deal.

It is meant for Nepal (NPR, phone numbers with +977). Planned languages: English, Nepali, and Hindi.

---

## What it is for

- Buyers: cheaper used goods near them (phones, laptops, furniture, vehicles, and more).
- Sellers: take photos, set a price, and reach nearby buyers.
- Both: chat in the app, agree on a meetup, and stay safer than dealing only outside the app.

This is not full online checkout. Wallet, payments, and live maps are planned, not live yet.

---

## Account

- See a short intro: buy nearby, sell quickly, chat safely.
- Log in (phone). Sign up. Verify with an OTP.
- Optional sign-in via Google, Apple, or Facebook (shown, not fully live).
- Log out.
- Edit profile, privacy, saved addresses (placeholders for later).

---

## Browse and buy

- Time-based greeting and a feed of deals.
- Search for items.
- Filter results.
- Browse by category (mobiles, laptops, electronics, furniture, vehicles, fashion, health, books, kids, sports, home, pets, music, business, and others).
- See trending and recently listed items, with distance.
- Open an item: photos, title, price, location, description.
- Save / unsave (wishlist / favorites).
- Get notifications (messages, price, saved items, offers, sold, and similar).

---

## Sell

- Post an ad in three steps.
- Add photos (up to 10) from the gallery; crop and compress.
- Pick category and condition: New, Good, or Fair.
- Enter title, description, and price.
- Choose where to meet: public place, seller location, or buyer location.
- See a success message after posting.
- View own listings (active, pending, sold).
- Edit a listing.

---

## Chat and trust

- List of conversations (including support).
- Message a seller or buyer and negotiate.
- Confirm an in-person meetup.
- Rate and review after a deal.
- Report or block a user.
- Safety tips: meet in public, inspect before paying, keep talk in the app.

---

## Profile and settings

- Profile with sold / bought counts and rating.
- Purchases history vs own listings.
- Saved items (wishlist).
- Light, dark, or system appearance.
- Language and currency options (English / Nepali / Hindi; NPR as default) — planned.
- Help: account, payments, safety, report a problem.
- Terms, privacy policy, contact support.
- Wallet, payment methods, and full transaction history — planned, not live.

---

## Bottom navigation

Users move around with five main areas:

1. **Home** — deals, search, categories  
2. **Explore** — browse by category  
3. **Sell** — post an item  
4. **Chats** — messages  
5. **Profile** — account and settings

## Not live yet

## Summary
